Privacy Policy

E-Approval Binzik — Digital Memo Approval

📅 Last updated: May 2026
1

Introduction

Welcome to E-Approval Binzik ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account information — username and employee data used for login and identity within the organization.
  • Memo data — content, attachments, approver lists, and recipients associated with memos you create or process.
  • File attachments — documents (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV) and images you upload as memo attachments, stored locally on your device.
  • Approval activity — records of approvals, rejections, and notes you submit within the approval workflow.
  • Device information — basic device data for app compatibility and error reporting.
3

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to operate and improve the App's core functionality:

  • To authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • To process, route, and track memo approvals within your organization.
  • To store and display file attachments associated with memos.
  • To send in-app notifications about pending approvals and memo status changes.
  • To improve app performance and fix technical issues.
4

Data Storage

All memo data and file attachments are stored locally on your device. The App does not currently transmit your personal data or attachments to external servers. Future versions may include cloud sync functionality, which will be disclosed in an updated Privacy Policy prior to release.

5

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Memo content may be visible to other users within your organization as part of the normal approval workflow (e.g., approvers can see memo content submitted to them).

6

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. File attachments are stored in the app's sandboxed local storage, accessible only by the App. We recommend keeping your device secure with a PIN or biometric lock.

7

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review the data associated with your account.
  • Delete locally stored attachments at any time through the App.
  • Request deletion of your account data by contacting us.
8

Children's Privacy

E-Approval Binzik is intended for use by employees within an organization. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated via an in-app notification. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
